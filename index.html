4. HOT assessment — suspicious item

Use HOT as a prompt for a suspicious item: Hidden, Obviously suspicious, Typical. Do not touch, move or investigate a suspected item. Follow local security procedures and notify the appropriate control room / police.

H — Hidden

O — Obviously suspicious

T — Typical

5. WHAT security assessment — railway

Use this structured prompt to describe suspicious behaviour in a railway environment. Record observable facts, avoid assumptions, and follow your organisation’s security and escalation procedures.

W — What are they doing?

H — How are they behaving?

A — Are they alone or with others?

T — What threat do they pose?
